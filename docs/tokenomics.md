## Disclosure rules

1. Each figure is published with its source and moves from *not yet stated* to *stated* only when the source exists.
2. Supply is verified against the deployed contract, not against a document.
3. Any allocation table will add up, and will name who receives what and on what schedule.
4. Utility will be described only as far as it is live. Proposed utility will be labelled as proposed.
5. A token does not by itself represent ownership of an underlying asset or a right to revenue, unless the published terms say so.
