# Equator: overview and vision

Version: draft. This document describes intent and publishing principles. It does not state contract terms, parameters or returns, because none have been announced.

## Vision

Equator aims to give holders of digital equities a structured way to put part of their upside to work, in options markets on Robinhood Chain. The working line from the project's own materials is: *turn a portion of your asset upside into potential premium.* The goal is a clearly bounded trade-off that a holder can read in full before acting.

Robinhood Chain is the settlement environment. Equator claims no affiliation with or endorsement by Robinhood Chain.

## Principles

**Every fact has a state.** A fact is *stated*, *not yet stated* or *unconfirmed*. The website and these documents follow the same rule, so a reader can always tell what is confirmed.

**Nothing is invented.** Figures, addresses and links appear only after they are stated. Unknown values are shown as an em dash, never estimated.

**Trade-offs in view.** Any product terms, once published, will state what a participant gives up as plainly as what they may gain. Market losses are possible, and nothing here is a guarantee of income.

**No custody by the website.** The site does not connect wallets, request signatures or store account data.

## What is not yet stated

- Contract addresses and deployment details
- Product parameters, cycle terms and fees
- Audit status: no completed independent audit is claimed
- Token utility and distribution (see [tokenomics](tokenomics.md))
- Official social channels

## Risks

Digital assets can lose value. Smart contracts can contain defects. Chain availability, liquidity and counterparty demand can affect outcomes. Read the published terms and verify addresses against every official channel before you interact with anything.

This document is not financial advice.
